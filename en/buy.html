Create Your Company is a strictly educational book. Its goal is to introduce children and teenagers to the basics of entrepreneurship, financial education, accounting and management, building creativity and critical thinking skills for adult life.

This book does not promote or encourage child labor. On the contrary, it recognizes and respects the right of all children and teenagers to a full childhood, to play, to education and to their overall development, in accordance with current law and the Convention on the Rights of the Child.

Before you start

What age is it for?

It's designed for kids ages 10 to 14. It's also lovely to read and play together as a family or with friends.

Are there bulk discounts?

Yes. Discounts start at 10 copies, with the Printed packs and a per-copy price based on quantity.

Is it available in other languages?

We're working on translating the book into other languages.

If you'd like it in a particular language, please write to us. We'd love to hear from you and let you know when that version is available.

libro@creatuempresa.ai

Is the book inclusive?

Yes. It was designed so every child can enjoy playing at creating their business, respecting their own pace, interests and ways of learning. It offers clear explanations, visual support, hands-on activities and a step-by-step path. Its approach aims to include children with different learning profiles, such as dyslexia, dyscalculia, autism (ASD), ADHD and 2E (twice-exceptional) profiles.

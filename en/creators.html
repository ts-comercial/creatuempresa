Why we made it

Because this is the book we wish we'd had as kids.

This book was born from seeing that many young people reach adulthood without the tools they need to take their first steps in the working world or to start a business.

We believe that giving them these tools early means more kids, when their time comes, can start businesses with confidence and build a future with more opportunities.

Shall we start the first-business game?

Six chapters, endless business ideas for inspiration and your own project designed by the time you finish reading.

Behind it

Creators Contact

Create Your Company is a strictly educational book. Its goal is to introduce children and teenagers to the basics of entrepreneurship, financial education, accounting and management, building creativity and critical thinking skills for adult life.

This book does not promote or encourage child labor. On the contrary, it recognizes and respects the right of all children and teenagers to a full childhood, to play, to education and to their overall development, in accordance with current law and the Convention on the Rights of the Child.

A financial education book for kids ages 10 to 14
